Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during the consultation?
We'll talk through your idea, your background and what you're hoping to explore. From there, we look at realistic options and map out practical next steps you can act on.
Do I need a fully formed idea to book?
Not at all. Many people come in with a general direction or just a sense that they want something different — that's a great place to start.
How should I prepare?
Just bring your experience, your ideas and your questions. If you have specific goals or constraints in mind, jotting a few notes beforehand can help us make the most of the time.
What happens after the conversation?
You'll leave with clarity on realistic next steps. There's no program or ongoing commitment — you decide what, if anything, to pursue from there.
